Authentic Spicy Thai Red Curry

35 MINS • EASY • SERVES 4

Ingredients

  • • 500g Chicken breast or thighs, thinly sliced
  • • 3 tbsp Thai Red Curry Paste (Maesri or Mae Ploy)
  • • 400ml Full-fat Coconut Milk
  • • 150g Bamboo shoots, sliced & drained
  • • 2 tbsp Fish Sauce (Nam Pla)
  • • 1 tbsp Palm Sugar (or brown sugar)
  • • 4-5 Kaffir Lime leaves, torn
  • • 1/2 cup Fresh Thai Basil leaves
  • • 2 Red bird's eye chilies, sliced (optional)
  • • 1 tbsp Neutral oil

Preparation

01

Heat the oil in a large wok or deep skillet over medium heat. Add the red curry paste and fry for 2 minutes until fragrant and the oil starts to separate. This "cracking" of the paste is essential for deep flavor.

02

Slowly pour in 1/2 cup of the coconut milk. Stir constantly until it incorporates with the paste and turns into a vibrant, thick red sauce. Add the sliced chicken and cook for 3-4 minutes until the outside is no longer pink.

03

Add the remaining coconut milk and the bamboo shoots. Bring to a gentle simmer. Do not let it boil vigorously, as the coconut milk might split.

04

Season with fish sauce and palm sugar. Add the torn kaffir lime leaves. Simmer for another 5-7 minutes until the chicken is tender and fully cooked.

05

Taste the sauce. It should be spicy, salty, and slightly sweet. Adjust seasoning if needed. Remove from heat and stir in the Thai basil and fresh chilies. Serve immediately over hot jasmine rice.

info Allergen Notice: Contains Fish Sauce. Substitute with light soy sauce or salt for a vegetarian-friendly alternative.
